# Guides

<p class="lead">Short how-tos, one task each, in the same groups as the menu.</p>

## Maps

- [Style the roads](style.md): palettes, base maps, labels, arrows, 3D bridges and the road classes to show.
- [Colour by your data](colour.md): colour the roads by a column, with your own colours, or with a menu of colourings.
- [Which road is on top](levels.md): bridges, tunnels and ramps in the right order, and an editor to fix a place by hand.
- [Add your own layers](overlays.md): zones, points and lines under or over the roads.
- [Roads from OpenStreetMap](osmnx.md): no data of your own? Download a place with osmnx and draw it.
- [Big networks](big-networks.md): vector tiles in the file, for a city or a region.
- [Studio](../studio.md): make a map with knobs, with no code.

## Street View and 3D

- [Street View](street-view.md): click a road and see it in Google Street View, Panoramax or Mapillary.
- [Google 3D](google-3d.md): Google's 3D view in a window, its camera linked with the map's.
- [Keys and tokens](google-key.md): the Google key and the Mapillary token.

## Dashboards

- [Dashboards](dashboards.md): a map with a toolbox and a panel, from one Python call.
- [Layout and toolbox](dashboard-layout.md): what each tool and window of a dashboard does.
- [Dashboard parts](dashboard-parts.md): the parts of the panel, each one live, with its code.
- [JavaScript](dashboard-manual/javascript.md): control a dashboard from JavaScript, and live data.
- Editors and tools (each one needs JavaScript in the page):
    - [Edit the design of a map](design-panel.md): colours, widths, labels and themes, with a live map.
    - [Edit the levels](levels.md#edit-the-levels-in-a-dashboard): which road is on top, place by place.
    - [The data dock](data-dock.md): SQL on your database, with the result under the map.
    - [Ask](ask.md): questions about the data to an AI model, with the person's own key.

## Use it elsewhere

- [On a website or in a map app](website.md): the saved page in an iframe, or a style for other map apps.
- [With AI agents](agents.md): let an AI agent make maps with roadstyle.
